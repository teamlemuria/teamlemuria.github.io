Our story

Lemuria Nursery is the largest wholesale grower in Solano County, open to the public. At Lemuria, we grow hundreds of varieties of plants. Our list of plants is always evolving. Many cultivars are collected locally and propagated onsite. At our nursery, you will find a great selection of hard to find Fruit Trees, Shade Trees, Roses, Shrubs, Perennials and Ornamental Grasses. We also grow some UC Davis Arboretum selections, a good assortment of succulents and even a few aquatic plants.
